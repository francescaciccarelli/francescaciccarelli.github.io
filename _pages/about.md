<h2 class="home-section-title">About Me</h2>

<p>
I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Information and Communication
Technologies at the University of L'Aquila, Italy.
</p>

<p>
My research focuses on <strong>Machine Unlearning</strong>,
within the broader field of <strong>Trustworthy AI</strong>.
I am particularly interested in understanding how machine
learning models can forget information and how to evaluate
the effectiveness of this process.
</p>
